How can we help?

Welcome to Rovsta, your city discovery app. If you have a question, encounter a problem, or want to share feedback, we're here to help.

Contact support

Email us and include a short description of the issue. If relevant, mention your device model and iOS version. Please do not include passwords or other sensitive information.

Email Rovsta Support

Support email: rovsta.app@gmail.com

What to include in your message

  • What you were trying to do in Rovsta.
  • What happened, including any error message.
  • Your device model and iOS version, if relevant.
  • Steps we can follow to reproduce the issue, if possible.

Feedback and suggestions
